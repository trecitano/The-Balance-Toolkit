Specification

The values shown are the defaults. Rows marked Configurable can be changed in the settings or for each session.

BoardNintendo Wii Balance Board (RVL-WBC-01)
Boards per session1 or 2Configurable
Sensors4 load cells: top right, bottom right, top left, bottom left
Board rateAbout 100 Hz
Resampling100 Hz with cubic interpolation; linear and polynomial also availableConfigurable
Analysis window5000 ms, sliding every 100 ms (about 10 results per second)Configurable
Sensor spacing446 × 238 mmConfigurable
MetricsCentre of pressure, CoP velocity, stability index, dynamic postural stability indices
RecordingCSV with RFC 3339 timestamps; raw and processed data can each be turned off; output folderConfigurable
TCPlocalhost:11223 raw, localhost:11224 processed; addresses and each streamConfigurable
LSLStream name and source ID the-balance-toolkit; raw and processed streamsConfigurable
ActivitiesSteps, durations and repeats can be edited, or reset to the defaultConfigurable
Demo modeA simulated board, to try the toolkit without oneConfigurable
PlatformsWindows, macOS and Linux; Android companion

Activities

Each activity is a sequence of steps. Each step has its own drawing and a countdown.

  • Eyes open-close

    Quiet standing with eyes open, then closed

    3 steps · 0:40 · 1 board

  • Functional Reach Test

    Reaching forward while keeping balance

    8 steps · 2:24 · 1 board

  • Single leg stance

    Left leg up, then right, with rests between

    6 steps · 2:08 · 1 board

  • Tandem stance

    Feet heel to toe, each foot in front in turn

    6 steps · 2:08 · 1 board

  • Timed Up and Go

    Sit, stand, walk, turn, walk back, sit

    6 steps · 2:24 · 1 board

  • Squat

    Controlled weight shift across two boards

    4 steps · 0:24 · 2 boards
