The board is your controller

The Balance Toolkit streams a Wii Balance Board’s centre of pressure into your game over TCP or LSL. Try it: keep the red dot inside the green ring for 30 seconds.

The ring drifts. Your weight is the pointer, smoothed like a body. Scores are seconds inside the ring.

Six levels, built in

The toolkit ships six guided activities. Each one runs a timeline of steps with an illustration, and records the whole thing.

Read it in a few lines

Every sample goes out as a 40-byte packet. Unity, Godot, Python or anything with a socket can read it. LSL streams line it up with EEG or eye tracking.

TCP raw 11223TCP processed 11224LSL basic · complex
sock = socket.create_connection(("::1", 11223))
while True:
    t, mac, tr, br, tl, bl, x, y = \
        struct.unpack(">qQ6f", sock.recv(40))
    player.lean(x, y)   # your game here
