Standing still is hard work

A balance lab from a game accessory. The Balance Toolkit turns the Wii Balance Board into a research instrument.

It pairs the board on Windows, macOS and Linux, measures sway with the metrics clinicians report, and streams it live.

Download the apps

The problem

Force plates cost a lab. This board was a toy.

Even when you think you are standing still, you are not. Your centre of pressure wanders a few millimetres every second, and how far it wanders says something about your balance. Measuring it usually takes a laboratory force plate. The Wii Balance Board has four load sensors, one in each corner, and that is enough to compute the same thing.

We asked 26 balance and rehabilitation experts in six regions what kept them from using the board. Most named the same few things.

17/26named cost as a barrier to balance equipment
13/18board users had trouble pairing it over Bluetooth
9/18lost the Bluetooth connection during use

So the toolkit starts there: pairing that works, one or two boards in a session, six guided activities, and the centre-of-pressure and stability metrics clinicians already report, written to plain CSV and streamed over TCP and LSL.
