1 Motivation

Force plates measure balance well but cost a laboratory budget. The Wii Balance Board has four load sensors and is already in many homes, but pairing it and getting usable data out of it is hard.

2 What experts told us

26 balance and rehabilitation experts in 6 regions answered our survey.

Named cost as a barrier 17 / 26
Board users who struggled to pair 13 / 18
Board users who lost Bluetooth in use 9 / 18
